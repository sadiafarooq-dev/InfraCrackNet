ABOUT US

Built to Catch Damage Before It Spreads

InfraCrackNet is a final year project at COMSATS University Islamabad, built to make crack inspection for roads and buildings faster, more consistent, and easier to track from first capture (photo or video) to final fix.

OUR MISSION

Catching problems while they're still small

Cracks in roads and buildings are usually reported too late, after they have already become expensive or dangerous. InfraCrackNet gives inspection teams a simple way to capture a photo or video, get an instant AI read on severity, and route it to the right engineer, so small problems get fixed while they are still small.

Is There a Crack?

The first model looks at a photo or video frame and decides whether there's a crack at all, with a confidence score attached.

{{ icon_road('step-icon') }}
What Kind of Crack?

For road surfaces, the second model classifies the type of damage it's looking at, so engineers know what they're dealing with.

{{ icon_gauge('step-icon') }}
How Bad Is It?

The third model traces the crack's actual shape and estimates its severity (Low, Medium, or High) with a real measurement in centimeters when possible.
