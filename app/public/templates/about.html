ABOUT US

Built to Catch Damage Before It Spreads

InfraCrackNet is a final year project at COMSATS University Islamabad, built to make crack inspection for roads and buildings faster, more consistent, and easier to track from first photo to final fix.

OUR MISSION

Catching problems while they're still small

Cracks in roads and buildings are usually reported too late, after they have already become expensive or dangerous. InfraCrackNet gives inspection teams a simple way to capture a photo, get an instant AI read on severity, and route it to the right engineer — so small problems get fixed while they are still small.
