SUPPORT

Help & FAQ

Answers to common questions about using InfraCrackNet.

GENERAL
What does InfraCrackNet actually do?

It looks at a photo or video of a road or building, uses AI to check for cracks, classifies the type and severity, and routes the finding to an Engineer for a real professional decision.

Is the AI's result final?

No. Every AI finding is reviewed by a human Engineer before anything is acted on. The AI supports the decision, it doesn't replace it.

FOR INSPECTORS
Can I upload a video instead of a photo?

Yes. The system samples frames from the video automatically and runs each one through the same detection pipeline.

How do I know if my report was reviewed?

Your report's status updates live. Once an Engineer finishes, it moves to "Reviewed" and you'll see their notes right on the report.

FOR ENGINEERS
Can I override what the AI found?

Yes. The Report Review screen lets you confirm, correct, or fully override the AI's crack type, severity, and suggested treatment.

What is a Follow-up Scan?

After a report is reviewed, you can add a new photo of the same spot. It runs through the same AI pipeline and shows a before/after comparison on the same report thread.

FOR ADMINS
How do Inspectors and Engineers get accounts?

You can add them directly from User Management, or they can sign up themselves with a registration code you issue.

Where do I see system-wide activity?

The Overview and Trends dashboards show stats and patterns across every report, and the Audit Log tracks key account and status changes.
