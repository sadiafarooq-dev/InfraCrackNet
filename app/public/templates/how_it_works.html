HOW IT WORKS

From First Capture to Final Fix

Whether it's a quick road photo or a full building video walkthrough, every report follows the same path from the field to a decision.

STEP ONE

Two Ways to Capture

{{ icon_road('step-icon') }}

Road: A Quick Photo

For a road surface, the Inspector just snaps a photo on site, pins the location, and adds a couple of quick notes. That's it. No extra setup.

{{ icon_building_floor('step-icon') }}

Building: A Video Walkthrough

For a building, the Inspector can upload a short walkthrough video instead. InfraCrackNet reads through the footage, estimates which floor each part was filmed at, and picks out the clearest frame of the crack automatically.

STEP TWO

Then, One Process for Both

{{ icon_magnifier('step-icon') }}
AI Analyzes It

Three AI models check whether there's a crack, classify the damage, and trace/measure its severity, in seconds.

{{ icon_clipboard('step-icon') }}
An Engineer Reviews

The findings land in the Engineer's Review Queue, where they confirm or override the AI and set the cause, treatment, and status.

{{ icon_check('step-icon') }}
Everyone Tracks It to Resolution

The Inspector sees the decision on their report, and Admin can watch trends and follow-ups across the whole system.
