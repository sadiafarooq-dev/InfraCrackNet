HOW IT WORKS

From First Photo to Final Fix

1
Inspector captures a photo

On site, the Inspector uploads a photo or short video of the crack, pins the location, and answers a few quick context questions.

2
AI analyzes it instantly

Three AI models check whether there is a crack, classify the damage type, and trace/measure its severity — in seconds.

3
Engineer reviews and decides

An Engineer checks the AI findings, confirms or overrides them, and sets the cause, treatment, and status.

4
Everyone tracks it to resolution

The Inspector sees the decision on their report, and Admin can watch trends and follow-ups across the whole system.
