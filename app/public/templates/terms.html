Terms & Privacy

How we handle your data, and what using InfraCrackNet means for you.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By creating an account or using InfraCrackNet, you agree to these terms. This platform is a Final Year Project built for academic evaluation and demonstration. It is not a commercial production service.

2. Who Can Use This Platform

Access is limited to Inspectors, Engineers, and Admins within an organization, using an account created directly by an Admin or signed up with a valid registration code.

3. What Data We Collect

We collect the account details you provide (name, email), photos and videos you upload for inspection, on-site notes you add, and the AI findings and Engineer decisions tied to each report.

4. How We Use Your Data

Uploaded photos are analyzed by our trained AI models to detect and classify cracks. This data is used only to generate inspection reports and system-wide trend analytics for your organization, never sold or shared externally.

5. AI Findings Are Decision Support

AI results (crack presence, type, severity) are shown as a starting point for a human Engineer's review. They are not a final judgement on structural safety. See each report's Engineer Assessment for the final decision.

6. Data Retention

Reports and their photos are kept for as long as your organization's account is active, so the full history of each site's inspections stays available for tracking repairs over time.

7. Your Rights

You can review, correct, or request removal of your own account details at any time from your Profile settings, or by contacting your Admin.

8. Questions

For anything not covered here, reach out to your Admin directly.
